# Privacy

MyCommandCenter runs inside your own Muse AI and your own accounts. This file says exactly where things live and what never happens.

## Where your data lives

- The board and everything on it: inside your Muse, in the board app's own storage. Only you can open it. It is not published, not shared, and has no public link.
- Email Brief runs and files: in your Muse workspace. The brief reads your connected inboxes, read-only.
- Bridge files: in a folder in your own Google Drive, if you set the Bridge up. The snapshot there is a copy of your board's contents. Anyone with access to that Drive folder can read it, so treat the folder like the board itself: do not share the folder unless you mean to share the board's contents. Your Muse will never share it for you (Operating Rules, rule 9).
- File Save Log: the scanner runs on your own computer; the master log and full-log file live where you chose in setup (your Drive, or a local file in your Muse workspace). The log's recorded content is the names and full paths of files saved in your watched folders, and a file name can itself be sensitive, for example a client name or a case name. Choosing the watched folders and the log location is therefore a privacy choice, and setup makes it with that in mind.

## What never happens

- No telemetry. Nothing in this kit phones home, reports usage, or sends your board, your name, or your data to the kit's authors or anyone else.
- The kit's authors receive nothing automatically. Feedback (FEEDBACK.md) is written by you, reviewed by you, and sent by you, only if you choose to.
- Muse never sends email for you, posts for you, or shares your board. It can prepare a draft; sending is yours.
- Upgrades do not reach into your board on their own. An upgrade runs only when you paste the upgrade prompt, and it backs your board up first.

## What the kit itself contains

Structure, instructions, and blank templates. No one's real data, tasks, names, or links are in this kit, yours will be the first data your board ever holds.

## Your controls

- Export your board at any time: ask your Muse to save the full board (getdashboard) as a file for you.
- Delete the board at any time: ask your Muse. Deletion is permanent, so your Muse will confirm with you by name first, and offer the export before it deletes.
- Disconnect any account (email, Drive) at any time in your Muse's settings. The board keeps working; the module that used the account pauses.
