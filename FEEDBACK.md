# Feedback

Feedback is voluntary. Nothing is collected automatically (see PRIVACY.md). If you send feedback, you write it, you review it, you send it.

## The easiest way

Ask your Muse: "Prepare my MyCommandCenter feedback." It will draft a short summary from what you tell it, show it to you for edits, and then you send it by whatever channel you were given with the kit (email or form). Do not send board contents you do not want to share; a good bug report needs the step, what you expected, and what happened, not your data.

## Check-in questions (used in pilots at day 1, 7, and 30)

Day 1, install:
- Where did you have to guess, or re-read?
- Which step took longest, and why?
- Anything Muse built that was not what the guide described?

Day 7, first week:
- Which panels have you actually used? Which have you not opened?
- How accurate was the Email Brief (misses, noise, timing)?
- How many items are sitting in your Parking Lot?
- Is Muse's proactivity level (your tier) right, too much, or too little?

Day 30:
- Keep, change, or drop, per module: board and rail, Email Brief, Bridge, daily reviews, Recent File Saves.
- Your honest estimate: how much time a week does this save you, if any?
- Would you recommend it to someone like you? What would you tell them first?

## Bugs worth reporting any time

- Muse invented a date, estimate, name, or link (Operating Rules, rule 3).
- Muse marked something done by itself (rule 2), or deleted something without confirmation (rule 1).
- The same task or item appeared twice (rule 4).
- A deleted Recent File Saves entry came back after a full cycle.
- A Bridge request was applied twice, or applied differently from what the file said.
