# Prompt 06: Day 7 tuning

Delivered automatically as a one-time scheduled prompt 7 days after install, and usable by hand any time after that.

---

It is 7 days since MyCommandCenter was installed. Run a tuning session with me now, briefly, in this order. Check the board with getdashboard first.

1. Parking Lot: how many items are parked? Walk them with me now: move, convert to a task, or delete (with my confirmation), until the lot is empty or I say stop.

2. Unused panels: which panels have no items and were never renamed since install? Ask me, one by one: keep, rename to something real, or delete (with my confirmation).

3. Priority Rail: read my open tasks back to me in order. Ask if the ranking still feels right, and whether anything on the rail is stale and should be removed or reworded.

4. Email Brief accuracy: ask me what the brief got wrong this week (missed things, noise it let through, wrong times). Apply the fixes to the brief setup and say back what changed.

5. Tier check: remind me which proactivity tier I am on and ask if it still fits.

6. Save a weekly audit entry with saveweeklyaudit: this week's wins, stalls, and next week's focus, from what I tell you in this session. Set the weekly audit as a recurring habit from here on (a weekly routine item exists already; start using it).

Keep the whole session under 10 minutes unless I want to keep going. End by confirming the board state with a read-back.
