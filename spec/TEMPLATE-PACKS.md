# Template Packs

Version: current kit version (see the VERSION file at the kit root)

One core board, three starter packs. A pack changes only: panel display names, the routine catalog (ROUTINE-CATALOG.md), and the recurring panel's display name. A pack never changes the data model, the actions, or the rules. A user can rename anything a pack set, at any time, and can mix packs by renaming panels by hand. Muse should say this during the interview.

## Template panels (the ten blank panels, before pack adjustments)

In default order, after the fixed panels (recurring panel, Quick Open, Administrative, Parking Lot, and File Saves History, which is created only when the File Save Log module is set up and sits second when it exists):

1. Projects
2. Clients and Customers
3. Meetings and Follow-ups
4. Team and People
5. Vendors and Partners
6. Marketing and Sales
7. Operations
8. Learning
9. Personal and Family
10. Ideas and Someday

All ten seed blank: no items, no links. During the interview, panels are renamed from the user's own areas of work and life (INTERVIEW.md question 3). Panels no answer covers keep these default names.

## Pack: Solo / Very Busy Individual (default)

- Recurring panel name: Recurring Activities
- Panel adjustments: Personal and Family moves to position 6 (first template slot). All other template names unchanged.
- Intended for: one person juggling work and life, no team, no clients, or clients light enough that Projects covers them.

## Pack: Small Business Operator

- Recurring panel name: Operating Rhythm
- Panel adjustments: Clients and Customers stays at slot 2, Vendors and Partners stays at slot 5, and Administrative is seeded with blank starter checklist items (titles only, no amounts, no dates): Monthly money review, Payroll check, Tax paperwork, Insurance and licences, Bills to pay.
- Intended for: an owner or manager of a small company, with customers, vendors, and money routines.

## Pack: Consultant / Client Work

- Recurring panel name: Recurring Activities
- Panel adjustments: Clients and Customers is renamed Clients and Engagements. Marketing and Sales is renamed Pipeline and Proposals.
- Intended for: consultants, freelancers, and advisors whose work is a set of client engagements plus a pipeline of future work.
